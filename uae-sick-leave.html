No More Filing It Yourself: UAE Clinics Will Now Handle Your Sick Leave Attestation

⚡ What Changed for UAE Residents
  • Zero Manual Filing: Patients no longer need to submit their own sick leave attestation requests at MoHAP-licensed facilities.
  • Handled by Your Clinic: The hospital or clinic that issued the sick leave now files the attestation directly on your behalf.
  • Includes Companion Leave: The same automatic process also applies to patient companion leave certificates.

Patients in the United Arab Emirates will no longer have to file their own sick leave attestation requests at healthcare facilities licensed by the Ministry of Health and Prevention (MoHAP).

Under the newly streamlined workflow, the clinic or hospital that issued the sick leave certificate will now submit and handle the official attestation request directly on the patient’s behalf—removing the need for recovering patients to navigate online portals or upload medical paperwork themselves.
